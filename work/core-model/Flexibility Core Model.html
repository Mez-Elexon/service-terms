Reading the notation

The model uses a handful of symbols from set notation. Most of it is the language of lists, lookups and true/false tests that engineers already use. Each card gives the symbol, how to say it, what it is like, and an example taken from the model.

BSC symbols are kept where they already exist: K for a metering identity (Table X-4), j for a Settlement Period, i2 for a Secondary BM Unit and p for a party. Volumes keep the BSC’s run-together form, such as \(QM_{Kj}\). Every expression has an ID: D for definitions, E for rules and derived results, V for volumes, O for open items and objectives.

    Authority · Allocation · Participation

    Who can act

    Allocation is held in FMAR and is cross-market. Participation is each sub-market’s own record. Every participation must trace back to an allocation (E41); the dotted delegation route is finding F6.

    Measurement · BSC settlement

    Differencing in BSC settlement

    Click a cell to switch an AMSID Pair Allocation on or off in a Settlement Period. The MSID Pair is differenced whenever any AMSID Pair in the configuration participates (E45). Only the outermost participating AMSIDs are subtracted (E46), so A2 is never subtracted while A1 also participates. A change caused by another party notifies the MSID Pair participant (E47).

    Allocation · Objectives

    Switching without gaps

    Re-allocation at t* ends one allocation and starts the next at the same instant (D29, O3). Each participation sits inside the allocation it traces to (E41).
